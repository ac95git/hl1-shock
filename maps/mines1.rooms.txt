# mines1 -- the Pit Bottom. The campaign's first map, designed with Andrei on 2026-09-22.
#
# The shaft's foot is a worked-out chamber the company built into: a stepped void the cage
# descends through, three tiers of terrace round its walls, the yard on its floor. The player
# lands in overalls, is turned back at the drift by security ("suit up first"), finds the dry
# on the first terrace, takes suit and pick from their own locker, and goes through the
# decontamination airlock on the floor into the haulage way -- which is shaft1. shaft1's old
# workings climb back out into this map through the gate on the first terrace marked no entry;
# then the hoist takes the Heart, the kit is handed in at the dry, and the cage goes up. The
# ride is map two. This is NOT the game's hub (ROADMAP, "The structure"); it is the mine's.
# The terraces are the generator's honest version of a salt chamber: the volume widens as it
# rises, so each tier's rim is a walkable ledge of rock 192 wide with the drop inside it.

brief The bottom of the shaft, and the biggest room the player sees until the facility: a
  chamber cut when this was a salt mine, 3072 by 2048 and 1536 high, the company's pit
  bottom built into it on three tiers. The yard on the floor: the rail haulage with cars and
  a loco running east into the drift, the crusher and the conveyor climbing the north wall to
  the bin, the processing hall behind a window, the sump and its pump, the lamp room and the
  tool crib either side of the shaft. The first terrace, 512 up: the dry with the lockers,
  the workshop, the electrical room, the fan station in the west wall, and the gate to the
  old workings marked no entry. The second terrace, 1024 up: the foreman's office with its
  window over the drop, and the canteen. Steel bridges cross the void at both terrace
  heights. The shaft comes through the roof and the cage rides down in a mesh guide tower
  the full height of the chamber. Work light in pools, dark rock between, the crystal
  nowhere yet.
  Beats, in order. The map starts with the cage still descending: three seconds of rock,
  then the chamber opens under the player and the cage takes eighteen seconds to reach the
  floor. Nothing else happens in that time. On the floor, security at the drift door turns
  the player back until they are suited (the door is on a master the suit sets). The notice
  wall by the shaft says where the dry is. Up the yard stair to the first terrace, past the
  no-entry gate, east along the ledge to the dry: the player's own locker stands open with the
  suit and the pick; the HUD wakes when the suit goes on. Back down to the floor and into
  the airlock: the inner door opens now, the steam runs, the outer door opens onto the drift,
  and the drift is shaft1. Two or three Records on the way tell the crew and the missing man.
  Later, from shaft1's old workings, the player comes out behind the no-entry gate on the
  first terrace, opens it from that side, goes down to the hoist by the shaft, and the hoist
  takes the Heart and sends the cage up and back. Up to the dry: the kit is handed in (suit,
  pick, Shards), and only then does the cage button work. The cage rides up; a trigger at
  the top ends the map until map two exists.
  Stand-ins in v1: scientists and Barneys for the crew and security, vanilla sentence groups
  for their lines; the fan is a box Andrei replaces by hand; all transport is decoration;
  stock rock, steel, glass and chainlink. Lit with point lights until the emitting-texture
  pass in J.A.C.K.
entrance The cage, in the shaft above the chamber's roof, already descending. Facing east.
exit The same cage, after the hoist has taken the Heart and the kit has been handed in at the
  dry. The button in the cage rides it up; a trigger at the top of the shaft ends the map with
  a message until map two exists. And, twice, the level change into shaft1: through the
  airlock into the haulage way, and back from shaft1's old workings through the gate.

gate suitup hard: the airlock's inner door is on a master the suit sets; security says so
gate airlock hard: the outer door opens only after the decontamination cycle; story, not a puzzle
gate oldgate hard: the gate on the first terrace opens only from the old workings' side; seen locked at the start, opened from behind at the end
gate cage hard: the cage button is dead until the Heart is hoisted and the kit handed in; the story's order, not a puzzle
