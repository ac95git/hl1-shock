# shaft1 -- the cold open. The campaign's first map, grilled with Andrei on 2026-09-19.
#
# The bottom of the mine: the cage lands at the pit bottom station, the player takes suit and
# pick from their locker in the dry, the haulage way runs in and down past a junction to the
# face, the blast opens the chamber, the Heart is cut free, the way back collapses, the old
# workings climb out in the dark to a gallery above the station, the hoist takes the Heart,
# the kit is handed in at the dry, and the cage goes up. The ride is map two.
# docs/ROADMAP.md, "The shape of the game" and "The cold open" have the decisions; this file is
# the layout. Third cut, unattended, 2026-09-19 evening, from Andrei's notes on the second:
# bigger in both senses -- larger volumes so there is room for storytelling by hand, and more
# places with a reason each; the dry as the locker room used twice; the road before the miner
# long enough to foreshadow him; the crew and security scripted with stand-in lines.
# The station sits at x -1024..0 so the whole map fits inside the engine's +-4096.

map shaft1

brief A modern hard-rock mine, drill-and-blast, run by a company with more money than the ore
  explains. Drifts cut square, rock-bolted with mesh, steel sets where the ground is bad, dry
  and dusty, lit by work lamps strung on a cable so the light is a line of pools with dark
  between them. A rail haulage way with cars is the main road; the working drifts branch off
  it, narrower and lower. Hot and old at the bottom. The crystal shows as amber threads in the
  walls of the deepest drift, brighter the closer to the face. Behind the face is not mined
  space: a chamber that was arranged, tall and symmetrical, the crystal set in it on purpose,
  and wrong for a mine in every proportion.
  Beats, in order. The cage has just landed; the player is in overalls. The pit bottom station
  where the shift spends its day: two storeys, the lamp room and the tool crib below, the dry
  and the showers on the mezzanine, the foreman's office, benches, the notice wall. In the dry
  the player's own locker stands open with the suit and the pick in it; the HUD wakes when the
  suit goes on. The walk in under the lamps: the first leg, the junction where a second working
  drift runs north to an abandoned face and the pump room sits south over its sump, then down
  three legs with two bends, past the ventilation raise and the mesh-gated branches, to the
  face. The crew is there; one says the missing man has been gone too long (stand-in line); the
  foreman fires the blast and the chamber opens. Four deposits encase the Heart, one unstable,
  and mining at least one reaches it: the mining tutorial and the telegraph tutor, their Shards
  the first thing in the Grid. The Heart is a walk-over pickup, a unique Entry three Cells wide,
  not droppable; taking it brings the quake: the breach fills with rubble and the chamber's
  south wall opens into the old workings. The old workings are unlit, the flashlight's first
  job, and long: drips and wind first, then the missing man heard at intervals, far and then
  near (stand-in whispers), a flooded dip, the shrine in a dead end, then the vein where he
  waits (a zombie standing in for the maddened miner), and past him the ladderway up to his
  camp. The climbs bring the player out on the gallery above the station through the gate
  marked no entry they saw locked at the start. The hoist takes the Heart from the Grid and
  sends the cage up. Security sends the player on break: at the dry the kit is handed in, suit,
  pick and Shards (the strip flag), and only then does the cage button work. The ride is map
  two, and it begins with no suit.
  Stand-ins in v1: scientists and Barneys for the crew and security, vanilla sentence groups for
  their lines; the zombie; alien voices and the slaves' words for the incantations; stock rock,
  steel, chainlink and water. The look, the props, the posters and the signage are Andrei's in
  J.A.C.K.; crystal is amber.
  Code this map needs (built 2026-09-19): item_heart and its Item Type row; the hoist as a
  Station that takes a named Entry (stationtype 3); player_weaponstrip's flag 1 for the hand-in.
entrance The cage, west of the station, just landed. Facing east into the station.
exit The same cage, after the hoist has taken the Heart and the kit has been handed in at the
  dry. The button in the cage rides it up; a trigger at the top of the shaft ends the map with
  a message until map two exists.

gate rockface hard: the face is solid rock until the crew's blast opens it; story, not a puzzle
gate collapse hard: the breach fills with rubble when the Heart is taken; the old workings are the only way out
gate gallery hard: the gate on the gallery above the station opens only from the old workings' side; seen locked at the start, opened from behind at the end
gate cage hard: the cage button is dead until the Heart is hoisted and the kit handed in; the story's order, not a puzzle
