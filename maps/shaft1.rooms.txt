# shaft1 -- the cold open. The campaign's first map, grilled with Andrei on 2026-09-19.
#
# The bottom of the mine: the cage lands at the pit bottom station, the haulage way runs in and
# down to the face, the blast opens the chamber, the Heart is cut free, the way back collapses,
# the old workings climb out in the dark to a gallery above the station, the hoist takes the
# Heart. The player boards the next cage and the map ends; the ride up is map two.
# docs/ROADMAP.md, "The shape of the game" and "The cold open" have the decisions; this file is
# the layout. Second cut, after Andrei walked the first (2026-09-19): the haulage way longer,
# bent and descending 192; the station much larger with rooms off it; blind branches behind
# mesh; steel sets; the way back longer, climbing 352, and out through a gate on a gallery
# the player could see but not reach at the start.

map shaft1
message shaft1 -- the cold open
wall -0TNNL_RCK1
floor -0OUT_GRVL1
ceil -0OUT_RK3

brief A modern hard-rock mine, drill-and-blast, run by a company with more money than the ore
  explains. Drifts cut square, rock-bolted with mesh, steel sets where the ground is bad, dry
  and dusty, lit by work lamps strung on a cable so the light is a line of pools with dark
  between them. A rail haulage way with cars is the main road; the working drifts branch off
  it, narrower and lower. Hot and old at the bottom. The crystal shows as amber threads in the
  walls of the deepest drift, brighter the closer to the face. Behind the face is not mined
  space: a chamber that was arranged, tall and symmetrical, the crystal set in it on purpose,
  and wrong for a mine in every proportion.
  Beats, in order: the cage has just landed and the player is suited (Agility, their own) with
  the pickaxe; the pit bottom station where the shift spends its day (lamp room, tool crib, the
  foreman's office, benches, the notice wall); the walk in under the lamps, down three legs,
  past the stope and the mesh-gated branches; the crew at the face; the foreman fires the blast
  and the chamber opens; four deposits encase the Heart, one unstable, and mining at least one
  reaches it (the mining tutorial and the telegraph tutor; their Shards are the first thing in
  the Grid); the Heart is a walk-over pickup, a unique Entry three Cells wide, not droppable;
  taking it brings the quake: the breach fills with rubble and the chamber's south wall opens
  into the old workings; the old workings are unlit (the flashlight's first job), one attacker
  waits at a vein in the dark (a zombie standing in for the maddened miner), a shrine sits in a
  dead end, wordless; the old workings climb back and come out on the gallery above the
  station, through the gate marked no entry that the player saw locked at the start; the hoist
  takes the Heart from the Grid and sends the cage up; the player boards the cage and the map
  ends.
  Stand-ins in v1: scientists and Barneys for the crew and security, no voice; the zombie;
  stock rock, steel and chainlink. The look, the props, the posters and the signage are
  Andrei's in J.A.C.K.; crystal is amber.
  Code this map needs (built 2026-09-19): item_heart and its Item Type row; the hoist as a
  Station that takes a named Entry (stationtype 3); the strip flag that also removes the suit
  and empties the Grid is map two's.
entrance The cage, west of the station, just landed. Facing east into the station.
exit The same cage, after the hoist has taken the Heart. A button in the cage rides it up; a
  trigger at the top of the shaft ends the map with a message until map two exists.

gate rockface hard: the face is solid rock until the crew's blast opens it; story, not a puzzle
gate collapse hard: the breach fills with rubble when the Heart is taken; the old workings are the only way out
gate gallery hard: the gate on the gallery above the station opens only from the old workings' side; seen locked at the start, opened from behind at the end
